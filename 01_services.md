# Fractional CISO and Board Advisory

## Enabling secure, scalable engineering at executive level

I partner with CISOs, executive teams, and boards to help organizations manage cyber risk while continuing to move fast.

Working at the intersection of security leadership, engineering, and business strategy, I support organizations that need senior security judgement without adding full-time executive headcount. My focus is on making security credible, scalable, and aligned with business objectives, particularly where modern software delivery, platform engineering, and regulatory pressure collide.

My award-winning work in secure software delivery and DevSecOps has helped organizations improve risk posture, satisfy board and customer expectations, and reduce friction between security and engineering. I bring that experience directly into executive decision-making environments.

## Fractional CISO support

As a fractional CISO, I provide hands-on leadership, strategic oversight, and executive-level assurance without the overhead of a permanent role. Engagements are typically retained and tailored to your organization’s maturity, risk exposure, and growth trajectory.

Typical areas of support include:

- CISO cover or augmentation during periods of change
- Security strategy aligned to business and growth objectives
- Secure software delivery and product security leadership
- Executive and board-level risk communication
- Oversight of DevSecOps and secure SDLC transformation
- Regulatory and customer assurance support
- Coaching and development of internal security leaders
- Acting as a trusted sounding board for CISOs and VPs of Security

This model is particularly effective for scale-ups, PE-backed organizations, and companies operating in regulated or high-growth environments.

## Board and executive advisory

I advise boards and executive teams on material cyber risk, translating technical realities into clear, actionable insight that supports governance and informed decision-making.

Advisory support includes:

- Board-level cyber risk briefings and reporting
- Secure SDLC and product risk oversight
- Independent assessments of security posture and maturity
- Investment prioritization and budget justification
- Incident readiness and executive response coaching
- Alignment between security, engineering, and business leadership

My approach is pragmatic, evidence-based, and designed to build confidence at executive and board level.

## Secure SDLC and DevSecOps advisory

I help organizations design and mature secure software delivery practices that scale with modern engineering teams.

Support may include:

- Secure SDLC maturity assessments (BSIMM, SAMM, NIST SDF)
- DevSecOps strategy and roadmap development
- ISO 27001 control advisory for system development
- Security tooling and vendor selection strategy
- Customer product security RFIs and mediation
- DevSecOps talent strategy and leadership coaching

This work is often delivered as part of a broader fractional CISO or executive advisory engagement.

## Public speaking and executive workshops

I regularly speak at security and technology conferences, including as a keynote speaker, on DevSecOps, organizational change, and security leadership.

I also deliver private executive and board-level workshops on topics such as:

- Cyber risk ownership and governance
- Building effective security–engineering partnerships
- Scaling security culture
- Influencing without authority at senior levels
- Career progression and leadership in security

Sessions can be delivered virtually or onsite. Travel time and expenses are billed separately.

## Security tooling and product advisory

For security vendors and product teams, I offer independent, senior-level feedback on tools designed for engineering-led organizations.

This focuses on:

- Developer experience and adoption
- Workflow integration and friction
- Real-world security impact

Feedback can be delivered as written reports, recorded walkthroughs, or live advisory sessions.
