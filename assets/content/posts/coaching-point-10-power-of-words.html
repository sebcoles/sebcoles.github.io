Don't underestimate the power of words. A single sentence at the right time can shape an ego.

As a junior manager, you will have the opportunity to provide words that have more weight than you intend, so my junior management tip #10 is to ask yourself if the words you impart are worthy of a TTL of 10 years or more.

There are phrases that have echoed in my brain for over a decade. The people who said them will probably have no idea of the impact they had or even remember saying them.

"You have a talent for change management" led me to believe I had more to offer than being technical and shaped much of the rest of my career. "Revenue is vanity, profit is sanity, cash is reality" reminds me to focus on measuring what's important. "The greatest jihad is the jihad within" helps me to keep my ego in check.

As a junior manager, your words may also occupy space in a report for the next 10 years or more, or shape their identity, so choose them wisely. You might forget the conversation. They might not.
