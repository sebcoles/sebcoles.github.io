It is all about leverage.

Force multipliers. Return on investment. Cost benefit. Competitive advantage. The kernel. It is finding where you have leverage.

Coaching point #7 for junior managers is about consciously seeking and prioritising points of leverage.

One of the tactics of management is the nudge. The nudge is a comment, a point raised in a meeting, a question targeted at an assumption or belief in its forming stage, its idea stage, while it is still fragile and mouldable.

A nudge at the right time allows quick course correction. If the opportunity is missed and the belief solidifies, then a nudge no longer works and evolves into a challenge, which is more expensive for both parties.

As a manager, you will get more opportunities to see fragile ideas developing in your teammates or in projects of your peers. You also have licence to find them, even if they do not impact your team today.

That team meeting with your peers? That is not just about escalations. It is about finding points of leverage across the wider department. Opportunities to nudge things early, to help or protect your teams in 6-12 weeks' time.

One perfectly timed question can avoid an unnecessary distraction. It does not get much higher return on investment than that. One good requirement early can avoid a remediation programme.

That is leverage.

Your to-do list will always be too long. Do not optimise on items in done, optimise for leverage.
